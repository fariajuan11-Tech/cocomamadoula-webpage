Introduction

A calm, steady presence for the journey into motherhood.

My work is rooted in the belief that mothers deserve to be cared for with the same devotion they give to their babies. As a doula, I offer calm, steady, and respectful support through pregnancy, birth, and the postpartum period - tending not only to practical needs, but to the emotional and physical experience of becoming a mother.

I work slowly, intentionally, and with deep respect for each woman's body, intuition, and family culture. There is no one-size-fits-all approach here. Instead, I meet you where you are, offering guidance, reassurance, and experienced presence so you can move through this transition feeling supported rather than overwhelmed.

Your body knows. Your heart leads. You are not alone.

Newborn Care

Newborn care is tender and attentive. I help you understand your baby's rhythms, support feeding, sleep, and soothing, and bring a calming presence so your family feels supported in the early weeks. With over 10 years of experience as a Maternity Nanny, I bring practical wisdom alongside emotional steadiness. I have cared for families through those first fragile days and long nights. My approach blends hands-on guidance with reassurance, so you're not just getting through the newborn phase, you're learning to trust yourself in it.

About

How I hold and support your transition into motherhood.

I offer grounded, respectful support through pregnancy, birth, postpartum, and newborn care. My work combines traditional wisdom, practical guidance, and evidence-based care so you can feel safe, informed, and deeply accompanied in this transition.

I care for families in Spanish and English, with a calm and culturally sensitive approach that protects rest, honors your choices, and helps your home feel held during the early weeks.

Credentials

Education & Ongoing Formation

My work is grounded in traditional birth and postpartum wisdom, evidence-based care, and continued professional study. I am trained in pregnancy, birth, postpartum and newborn support, breastfeeding care, and emotional support for families.

Trained through direct study with Dr Michel Odent and Naoli Vinaver in the UK, and Aixa Laxmi and Laura Pertejo in Spain.

  • Care available in Spanish and English.
  • Pregnancy, birth, postpartum, and newborn support.
  • Breastfeeding support and emotional care for families.
