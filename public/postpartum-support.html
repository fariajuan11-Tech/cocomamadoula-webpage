Postpartum Support

Those early days are a whirlwind of love, exhaustion, feeding challenges, emotional shifts, and new rhythms. Many mothers feel overwhelmed, unsure, or disconnected from themselves.

What you need is not more Google advice - you need a steady, wise presence who can guide you with warmth and skill.

My role is to ease the load, answer your questions, support your healing, and help your baby settle into the world with gentleness.

Day & Night Care Gentle Recovery Family-Centered Support

What I Can Help You With

Imagine your early days feeling like this:

  • You are resting while someone you trust cares for your baby.
  • Feeding becomes clear and manageable.
  • Your emotional world feels supported.
  • Your home stays calm and functional.
  • You feel more confident every single day.
  • Nights feel less frightening and more predictable.
  • You are healing peacefully - not rushing, not overwhelmed.

How I Support You

  • Newborn care, settling, and soothing techniques.
  • Light household tasks.

Emotional Support

  • A safe space to decompress.
  • Guidance through baby blues or anxiety.
  • Reassurance and perspective.

Healing Support

  • C-section or vaginal birth recovery.
  • Rest planning.
  • Closing of the Bones ceremony.

Night Support

  • Overnight care.
  • Feeding assistance.
  • Creating safe, calm nighttime rhythms.
